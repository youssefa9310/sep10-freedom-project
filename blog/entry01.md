# Entry 1
##### 10/19/2026

For my Freedom Project I decided to choose **Mechanical Engineering**. I picked this because I’ve always liked figuring out how things work especially stuff like cars and engines and big machines with moving parts. I think it will be cool to learn about how engineers design physical things and solve problems in the real world. 

Before I chose mechanical engineering I was thinking about a few other ideas too. My backup topics were researching how big factory assembly lines operate and looking into how giant structures like skyscrapers and bridges are built. But I ended up going with mechanical engineering because it seems way more hands-on and interesting to me.

To start my research I checked out some websites to learn more about the topic. I looked at the [Bureau of Labor Statistics Mechanical Engineering Overview](https://bls.gov) to find out what engineers actually do every day at work and what kinds of jobs they can get. I also went to the [ASME - American Society of Mechanical Engineers Official Website](https://asme.org) to see some of the cool projects and new technology they are working on right now.
